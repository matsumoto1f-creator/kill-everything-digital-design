# Content bar

For short-form social content: reels, shorts, clips, carousels, and the writing around them.

**This is the thinnest part of the bar.** The web and video bars are built from taken-apart references with measured numbers. This one is built from a clip-selection gate and a few series rules. Extend it the way `THE-BAR.md` describes: add a reference, tag the claims, pull out rules and gates.

---

## Score every piece before it goes out

Judge each piece on five things. Score each 1 to 5, and write one line on the weakest.

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Does the first 1 to 2 seconds stop the scroll? |
| **Payoff** | Is there a clear punchline, emotional spike, or "wait, what" moment? |
| **Standalone clarity** | Does it make sense with zero context, and with the sound off? |
| **Length fit** | Is it tight for the platform? Cut anything before the payoff that is not the hook. |
| **Share trigger** | Would someone send this to a friend, and what would they say when they did? |

## Ship the best, reject the rest

- From every source video or batch, **post the top 2 or 3. Reject the rest.** If a video yielded ten clips, seven or more get cut.
- Never batch-post everything. Posting it all dilutes the account and teaches the algorithm the wrong thing. Scarcity plus quality wins.
- Pair each posted clip with a current sound where the platform rewards it.

## Rules for a series

- **One fixed shape per series,** repeated every episode, so a viewer recognises it in the first second. The shape is a design decision: write it down once and hold it.
- **Decide the call to action once, on purpose,** for the whole series. A series of know-how clips that sells indirectly ("show that you are the real deal, but not so much that you are giving it away") must have no call to action anywhere. A series that sells directly needs one clear next step. Do not mix them.
- **No named clients and no private details.** Show the know-how, not the engagement. Stories that touch client trust, security or a legal dispute are held back, and never used as hooks.
- **Numbers come only from your own notes.** Anything else is marked `[NEED NUMBER]` for the owner to fill.
- **Order the stories.** Run the strongest first. Hold the ones that read as failure until the series has earned them, and never as a hook.

## Writing

- **Cut AI-sounding writing.** Run copy through a slop check: stock openers, "in today's fast-paced world", lists of three adjectives, hedges, em-dash rhythm that every other model produces. Read it aloud. If it sounds like it came from a template, it did.
- **Short and specific.** The claim in the hook. One number. One concrete example. (Same rule as web copy.)
- **Take the structure of a viral post, never its words.** Grep the draft for the source's distinctive phrases before it ships.

## Gate G1 for content (pass/fail)

- [ ] Hook lands inside 2 seconds and is the biggest thing on screen
- [ ] One claim, one payoff
- [ ] Works with the sound off
- [ ] No client names, no private data, no unsourced numbers
- [ ] Series shape and call-to-action stance match the rest of the series
- [ ] Copy reads as written by a person
- [ ] It is one of the top 2 or 3 of its batch
- [ ] For video: `tools/check_video.py` passes and the frames have been read
